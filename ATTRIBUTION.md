## What this repository actually did

Both resources are well-known, broad surveys of the topics a front-end or
systems-design interview commonly covers (HTML/CSS/JS fundamentals for the
former; caching, load balancing, sharding, replication, and consistency for
the latter). Their **topic lists** were used as general orientation when
deciding what a specialty's question bank should cover — nothing more
specific was fetched or transcribed from either project in the course of
authoring this content.

Concretely, for every question in every bank:

- The prompt (`question.vi` / `question.en`), the scoring rubric
  (`expectedKeyPoints`, `rubric.criteria`), the difficulty classification
  (`difficulty_analysis`), and all multiple-choice options are **100%
  original authorship** — written from general subject-matter knowledge, not
  copied or closely paraphrased from either source.
- Neither source supplies answer keys, scoring rubrics, or difficulty tiers
  at all — h5bp deliberately ships questions without answers, and
  system-design-primer is topic prose, not a question bank — so this
  material could not have been adapted even if that had been the intent.

This attribution is included as a courtesy and out of respect for both
projects' contributors, consistent with the spirit of their CC BY licenses,
even though the relationship here is closer to "topical inspiration" than
"derivative work." If a future contributor does directly adapt specific
wording from either project, note that adaptation inline in the relevant
bank file and update this document accordingly.
